Safety & Compliance

Our unwavering commitment to the safety and security of every student.

Safety Is Our Foundation

At Zariya Transportation, safety isn't just a policy—it's our core principle. Every decision we make, from driver selection to vehicle maintenance, is driven by our commitment to the safety and well-being of the students we serve.

🛡️ Illinois State Compliance

We maintain strict adherence to all Illinois state transportation safety regulations and licensing requirements.

  • ✓Licensed Transportation Services Certification
  • ✓Illinois Department of Transportation Compliance
  • ✓Annual safety inspections and audits
  • ✓Compliance with all school district safety protocols

👥 Driver Vetting & Training

Our drivers are carefully selected, thoroughly vetted, and continuously trained to meet the highest standards.

  • ✓Comprehensive background checks
  • ✓Criminal history screening
  • ✓Driving record verification and insurance coverage
  • ✓Ongoing professional development and safety training

🚗 Vehicle Safety

Every vehicle in our fleet meets rigorous safety standards and is regularly maintained.

  • ✓ Modern, well-maintained vehicles
  • ✓ Regular mechanical inspections
  • ✓ Current insurance coverage
  • ✓ Clean, safe interiors
  • ✓ Safety equipment and emergency protocols

📋 Operational Standards

Every route, schedule, and interaction follows strict operational guidelines.

  • ✓ Documented pickup/dropoff procedures
  • ✓ Route planning and optimization
  • ✓ Real-time tracking and communication
  • ✓ Incident reporting and protocols
  • ✓ Emergency preparedness plans

🔍 Accountability & Monitoring

We maintain transparency and continuous oversight of all operations.

  • ✓ Regular route audits and observations
  • ✓ Parent feedback and surveys
  • ✓ School district partnerships
  • ✓ GPS and communication tracking
  • ✓ Performance evaluations and reviews
